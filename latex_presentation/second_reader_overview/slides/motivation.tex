\begin{frame}{Motivation}
\framesubtitle{The challenge of operational text evaluation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item LLMs increasingly produce open-ended text in operational settings.

    \item In these settings, evaluation must support decisions about individual outputs: accept, revise, rank, diagnose, or improve.

    \item A single holistic score can rank outputs, but does not explain \textit{why} an artefact is good or bad.

    \item To diagnose quality, ``good'' must be decomposed into dimensions that constitute the target construct.

    \item \textbf{Problem}: without explicit construct specification, multi-dimensional scores are difficult to interpret as meaningful measurements.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.30cm}

\begin{block}{Thesis motivation}
    Artefact-level evaluation requires explicit construct specification: defining quality dimensions, measuring them, and validating whether they support the intended quality claim.
\end{block}

\end{frame}
