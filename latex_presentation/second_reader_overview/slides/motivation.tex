\begin{frame}{Motivation}
\framesubtitle{The challenges of artefact-level evaluation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item LLMs are increasingly used to produce open-ended text in operational settings.
    
    \item Evaluation is therefore not only about comparing systems, but also about assessing individual outputs.
    
    \item System-level benchmarks ask: \textit{How capable is the model?}
    
    \item Artefact-level diagnostics ask: \textit{What is good or bad about this specific text?}
    
    \item \textbf{Problem}: ``good'' is not self-evident; it depends on which dimensions are taken to constitute quality.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.30cm}

\begin{block}{Thesis motivation}
    Artefact-level evaluation requires explicit construct specification: defining quality dimensions, measuring them, and validating whether they support the intended quality claim.
\end{block}


\end{frame}
