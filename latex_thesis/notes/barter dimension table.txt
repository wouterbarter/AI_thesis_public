| Criterion                    | Theoretical dimension | Rationale                                                                                                                                                                                          |                      Expected direction |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------: |
| **Brand Prestige**           |            **Reward** | Captures symbolic/reputational value of collaborating with a known or trusted brand.                                                                                                               |                                Positive |
| **Perceived Economic Value** |            **Reward** | Directly captures the monetary or premium value of the offered product/service/compensation.                                                                                                       |                                Positive |
| **Creator Brand Alignment**  |            **Reward** | Captures whether the deal enhances or fits the creator’s self-presentation and feed identity. It is a reputational/aesthetic reward, though low alignment could also be read as reputational cost. |                                Positive |
| **Functional Utility**       |            **Reward** | Captures practical usefulness of the offered product/service to the creator.                                                                                                                       |                                Positive |
| **Product Universality**     |            **Reward** | Captures general consumer appeal and broad relevance of the offering; more universal products are easier to value and easier to imagine using/content-producing around.                            |                                Positive |
| **Workload Volume**          |              **Cost** | Captures required time, effort, and production burden.                                                                                                                                             |                                Negative |
| **Creative Restrictiveness** |              **Cost** | Captures loss of autonomy and increased constraint from brand control.                                                                                                                             |                                Negative |
| **Call-to-Action Strength**  |     **Pitch Quality** | Captures warmth, enthusiasm, and invitational force in the text.                                                                                                                                   |                                Positive |
| **Rhetorical Objectivity**   |     **Pitch Quality** | Captures whether the pitch feels grounded and credible rather than hype-driven.                                                                                                                    | Usually positive, but likely non-linear |
| **Proposition Clarity**      |     **Pitch Quality** | Captures cognitive ease: whether rewards, requirements, and deliverables are immediately understandable.                                                                                           |                                Positive |
